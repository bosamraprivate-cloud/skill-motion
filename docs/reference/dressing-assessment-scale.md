# Scale for Assessing Functional Motor Skills — Dressing and Undressing Skills

> The original material has no step table for dressing. This scale is assembled only from text already in the project's documents, without new wording:
>
> - **Putting on (six garments):** the steps are the "Motor Planning" sentences for each garment in `correct-performance-guide.md`, in the same order. That section is the only one in the guide that gives the order of the actions.
> - **Functional domain** lists Motor planning plus every other section of the guide that has a sentence describing the same action for that garment.
> - **Functional sub-skills** quotes those sentences from the guide, prefixed with the section they come from.
> - **Taking off the jacket:** the 10 steps of Stage 4 in `project-details.md`. Only steps 3 and 4 have domains in the source (the "Fourth" example table and the Stage 6 analysis); the other steps are left untagged.
> - **Taking off the T-shirt, shirt, trousers, socks and shoes:** the steps of the project team's draft `undressing-steps-team-draft.md` (2026-11-14, written at the owner's request), **pending review by the project owners**. The guide has no undressing text for these garments, so these steps are untagged.
>
> The Score and Performance columns are left empty, as in the other scales.

## Dressing and undressing skill

### 1. Jacket

#### 1.1 Putting on the jacket

| Step No. | Step | Functional domain | Functional sub-skills | Score | Performance |
|---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Identifies the front and the back of the jacket | Motor planning | Motor planning: spatial planning |  |  |
| 2 | Opens the jacket and prepares it for putting on | Motor planning + Hand skills + Motor coordination | Hand skills: extends the hands to reach the jacket (reach); holds the edges of the jacket (grasp); lifts the jacket and moves it to the body (carry). Motor coordination: holds the jacket by both sides and spreads it out with both hands (symmetrical bilateral coordination) |  |  |
| 3 | Inserts the first arm into the sleeve and then the second arm | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Midline crossing | Range of motion: extends the arms backward; bends and extends the elbows; inserts the arms into the sleeves. Motor coordination: stabilises the jacket with one hand and guides the other arm into the sleeve (asymmetrical bilateral coordination); follows the sleeve opening and the position of the arm with the eyes (visual-motor coordination). Midline crossing: extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the sleeve on the opposite side |  |  |
| 4 | Pulls the jacket onto the shoulders and adjusts it | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Midline crossing + Hand skills | Range of motion: moves the shoulders to adjust the jacket; pulls the jacket onto the body. Motor coordination: inserts the arms and then adjusts the jacket on the shoulders (sequential motor coordination). Midline crossing: reaches with the hand to the edge of the jacket on the other side to adjust it on the body. Hand skills: lets go of the jacket after putting it on and adjusting it (release) |  |  |
| 5 | Fastens the jacket from bottom to top in order | Motor planning | Motor planning: sequential planning |  |  |

### 2. T-shirt

#### 2.1 Putting on the T-shirt

| Step No. | Step | Functional domain | Functional sub-skills | Score | Performance |
|---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Identifies the orientation of the T-shirt and holds it at the appropriate place | Motor planning + Hand skills + Motor coordination | Hand skills: extends the hand toward the T-shirt to reach it (reach); holds the two edges of the T-shirt with the fingers and the palm (grasp). Motor coordination: holds the two edges of the T-shirt with both hands to open it (symmetrical bilateral coordination) |  |  |
| 2 | Identifies the head opening and the sleeves before starting to dress | Motor planning | Motor planning: motor planning |  |  |
| 3 | Inserts the head through the opening intended for it, then inserts the arms into the sleeves | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Hand skills + Midline crossing | Range of motion: raises the arms upward; bends and extends the elbows; raises the head to insert it into the T-shirt opening; inserts the arms into the sleeves. Motor coordination: stabilises the T-shirt with one hand and guides the other arm into the sleeve (asymmetrical bilateral coordination); directs the sleeve opening toward the arm while following the movement with the eyes (visual-motor coordination). Hand skills: lifts the T-shirt and moves it toward the body (carry). Midline crossing: extends the right hand across the middle of the body to reach the sleeve on the left side, and vice versa |  |  |
| 4 | Pulls the T-shirt downward in a sequential, organised movement | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination | Range of motion: pulls the T-shirt downward. Motor coordination: inserts the arms and then pulls the T-shirt downward in sequential order (sequential motor coordination) |  |  |
| 5 | Adjusts the position of the T-shirt until it settles on the body | Motor planning + Midline crossing + Hand skills | Midline crossing: uses one hand to reach the edge of the T-shirt on the opposite side and adjust it. Hand skills: opens the hands and lets go of the T-shirt after securing it in place (release) |  |  |

### 3. Shirt

#### 3.1 Putting on the shirt

| Step No. | Step | Functional domain | Functional sub-skills | Score | Performance |
|---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Identifies the orientation of the shirt and its front and back | Motor planning + Hand skills + Motor coordination | Hand skills: extends the hand to reach the shirt and the buttons (reach); holds the edges of the shirt and the buttons using the fingers (grasp); lifts the shirt and moves it to the body (carry). Motor coordination: spreads out the shirt and holds its two sides with both hands (symmetrical bilateral coordination) |  |  |
| 2 | Inserts the arms into the sleeves in order | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Midline crossing | Range of motion: raises the arms; bends and extends the elbows; inserts the arms into the sleeves. Motor coordination: stabilises the shirt with one hand and inserts the other arm into the sleeve (asymmetrical bilateral coordination). Midline crossing: extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the opposite sleeve |  |  |
| 3 | Adjusts the shirt on the body | Motor planning + Range of motion | Range of motion: moves the hands in front of the body; brings the two sides of the shirt together |  |  |
| 4 | Begins fastening the buttons from top to bottom | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Midline crossing | Range of motion: fastens the buttons in order. Motor coordination: directs the buttons toward the buttonholes and follows their position with the eyes (visual-motor coordination). Midline crossing: reaches with one hand to the buttons on the opposite side of the shirt; reaches the opposite buttonhole to insert the button into it |  |  |
| 5 | Fastens the buttons one after another at a regular rhythm | Motor planning + Motor coordination + Hand skills | Motor coordination: inserts the buttons into the buttonholes in sequence using both hands (sequential motor coordination). Hand skills: lets go of the shirt after adjusting it and fastening the buttons (release) |  |  |

### 4. Trousers

#### 4.1 Putting on the trousers

| Step No. | Step | Functional domain | Functional sub-skills | Score | Performance |
|---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Identifies the orientation of the trousers and the leg openings | Motor planning | Motor planning: spatial planning |  |  |
| 2 | Holds the trousers and prepares them for putting on | Motor planning + Hand skills + Motor coordination | Hand skills: extends the hand to reach the trousers (reach); holds the two edges of the trousers (grasp); lifts the trousers and moves them toward the feet and the body (carry). Motor coordination: holds the two sides of the trousers with both hands and opens them (symmetrical bilateral coordination) |  |  |
| 3 | Inserts the first foot and then the second foot into the appropriate openings | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination | Range of motion: leans the trunk forward; bends the knees; lifts each foot to insert it into the trousers. Motor coordination: stabilises the trousers with one hand and guides the foot with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination); directs the foot toward the trouser opening while following it with the eyes (visual-motor coordination) |  |  |
| 4 | Pulls the trousers upward gradually until they reach the waist | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Midline crossing | Range of motion: extends the body to the upright position; pulls the trousers up to waist level. Motor coordination: inserts the feet and then pulls the trousers upward in sequence (sequential motor coordination). Midline crossing: uses the hand to reach the edge of the trousers on the other side while pulling them upward |  |  |
| 5 | Adjusts the position of the trousers around the waist | Motor planning + Midline crossing + Hand skills | Midline crossing: extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the opposite side of the trousers and adjust it. Hand skills: lets go of the trousers after adjusting them in place (release) |  |  |

### 5. Socks

#### 5.1 Putting on the socks

| Step No. | Step | Functional domain | Functional sub-skills | Score | Performance |
|---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Identifies the opening of the sock and its correct orientation | Motor planning + Hand skills + Midline crossing | Hand skills: extends the hand to reach the sock (reach); holds the two edges of the sock with the fingers (grasp). | 2 | Opens the sock and prepares it for inserting the foot | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Hand skills | Range of motion: opens the sock. | 4 | Pulls the sock gradually until it reaches its correct position | Motor planning + Motor coordination | Motor coordination: pulls the sock gradually until it covers the foot (sequential motor coordination) |  |  |
| 5 | Adjusts the position of the sock around the foot | Motor planning + Midline crossing + Hand skills | Midline crossing: reaches with the hand to the edge of the sock on the opposite side to adjust it. Hand skills: lets go of the sock after pulling it on and securing it on the foot (release) |  |  |

### 6. Shoes

#### 6.1 Putting on the shoes

| Step No. | Step | Functional domain | Functional sub-skills | Score | Performance |
|---:|---|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | Identifies the appropriate shoe for each foot | Motor planning + Hand skills + Midline crossing | Hand skills: extends the hand to reach the shoe (reach); holds the shoe by its edges (grasp). Midline crossing: extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the shoe located beside the opposite foot |  |  |
| 2 | Opens the shoe and prepares it for inserting the foot | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Hand skills | Range of motion: opens the shoe. Motor coordination: holds the shoe with both hands and opens it (symmetrical bilateral coordination). Hand skills: lifts the shoe and moves it toward the foot (carry) |  |  |
| 3 | Inserts the foot into the shoe in the correct way | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination | Range of motion: leans the trunk forward; bends the knee; lifts the foot; extends the hand toward the shoe; inserts the foot and adjusts the heel. Motor coordination: stabilises the shoe with one hand and guides the foot with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination); directs the foot into the shoe while following its position with the eyes (visual-motor coordination) |  |  |
| 4 | Adjusts the heel inside the shoe | Motor planning + Midline crossing | Midline crossing: reaches with the hand to the outer part of the shoe to adjust its position if it is on the opposite side |  |  |
| 5 | Fastens the shoe using the hook-and-loop strap or the laces | Motor planning + Range of motion + Motor coordination + Hand skills | Range of motion: fastens the shoe if it has a fastening mechanism. Motor coordination: adjusts the position of the shoe and fastens it if it has a lace or a hook-and-loop strap (sequential motor coordination). Hand skills: lets go of the shoe after putting it on and adjusting it (release) |  |  |
