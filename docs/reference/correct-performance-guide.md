## 1. Range of Motion

### First: Range of Motion within Dressing Skills

#### Putting on the T-shirt

- Raises the arms upward (range of motion of the shoulder joint)
- Bends and extends the elbows (flexion and extension of the elbow joint)
- Raises the head to insert it into the T-shirt opening (neck extension)
- Inserts the arms into the sleeves (shoulder extension, and elbow flexion and extension)
- Pulls the T-shirt downward (extension of the arms and movement of the shoulders)

#### Putting on the Trousers

- Leans the trunk forward (trunk flexion)
- Bends the knees (flexion of the knee joint)
- Lifts each foot to insert it into the trousers (flexion of the hip and knee joints)
- Extends the body to the upright position (extension of the trunk and knees)
- Pulls the trousers up to waist level (extension of the arms and movement of the shoulder joint)

#### Putting on the Jacket

- Extends the arms backward (extension of the shoulder joint)
- Bends and extends the elbows (flexion and extension of the elbow joint)
- Inserts the arms into the sleeves (extension and flexion of the arms)
- Moves the shoulders to adjust the jacket (movement of the shoulder joint)
- Pulls the jacket onto the body (extension of the arms and movement of the shoulders)

#### Putting on the Shirt

- Raises the arms (extension of the shoulder joint)
- Bends and extends the elbows (flexion and extension of the elbow joint)
- Inserts the arms into the sleeves (extension and flexion of the arms)
- Moves the hands in front of the body (extension of the arms and movement of the shoulder joint)
- Brings the two sides of the shirt together (adduction of the arms in front of the body)
- Fastens the buttons in order (flexion of the fingers and movement of the hand joints)

#### Putting on the Socks

- Leans the trunk forward (trunk flexion)
- Bends one knee (flexion of the knee joint)
- Lifts the foot in order to reach it (flexion of the hip and knee joints)
- Extends the hands toward the foot (extension of the arms and movement of the shoulder joint)
- Opens the sock (extension and abduction of the fingers)
- Inserts the toes and pulls the sock over the heel (movement of the toes, and extension and flexion of the arms)

#### Putting on the Shoes

- Leans the trunk forward (trunk flexion)
- Bends the knee (flexion of the knee joint)
- Lifts the foot (flexion of the hip and knee joints)
- Extends the hand toward the shoe (extension of the arm and movement of the shoulder joint)
- Opens the shoe (extension and abduction of the fingers)
- Inserts the foot and adjusts the heel (movement of the foot, and flexion and extension of its joints)
- Fastens the shoe if it has a fastening mechanism (flexion of the fingers and movement of the hand joints)

### Second: Range of Motion within Eating and Drinking Skills

#### Eating a Sandwich

- Holds the sandwich in the hand and raises it to mouth level (flexion of the shoulder and elbow joints)
- Brings the sandwich close to the mouth (elbow flexion and forward movement of the shoulder)
- Opens the mouth and takes the food (movement of the jaw and the mouth muscles)
- Moves the sandwich away from the mouth after taking the food (elbow extension and backward movement of the shoulder)

#### Eating with the Spoon

- Holds the spoon and lifts it from the plate (elbow flexion and wrist movement)
- Brings the spoon close to the food and scoops a suitable amount (movement of the shoulder, elbow, and wrist)
- Raises the spoon toward the mouth (flexion of the elbow and shoulder)
- Places the spoon inside the mouth and then takes it out (movement of the wrist and elbow)

#### Eating with the Fork

- Holds the fork in the hand (flexion of the fingers around the fork handle)
- Directs the fork toward the food (extension of the arm and movement of the shoulder and elbow)
- Presses the fork onto the food to pick it up (movement of the wrist and fingers)
- Raises the fork toward the mouth (flexion of the elbow and shoulder)
- Puts the food into the mouth and then moves the fork away (wrist movement and elbow extension)

#### Drinking with the Cup

- Reaches for the cup with the hand (extension of the shoulder and elbow)
- Holds the cup and lifts it (flexion of the fingers and flexion of the elbow)
- Brings the cup close to the mouth (elbow flexion and shoulder movement)
- Tilts the cup to drink (wrist movement and tilting of the cup)
- Returns the cup to its place (elbow extension and lowering of the arm)

#### Drinking with the Bottle

- Reaches for the bottle with the hand (extension of the shoulder and elbow)
- Holds the bottle with the fingers (flexion of the fingers)
- Raises the bottle toward the mouth (flexion of the elbow and shoulder)
- Tilts the bottle to drink (movement of the wrist and forearm)
- Lowers the bottle and returns it to its place (elbow extension and lowering of the arm)

#### Drinking with the Straw

- Holds the cup and stabilises it with the hand (flexion of the fingers and wrist movement)
- Brings the cup close to the mouth (flexion of the shoulder and elbow)
- Places the straw inside the mouth (movement of the hand and wrist)
- Maintains the position of the cup while drinking (stabilisation of the arm and wrist)
- Moves the cup away from the mouth and returns it to its place (elbow extension and lowering of the arm)

### Third: Range of Motion within Personal Hygiene Skills

#### Handwashing

- Extends the hands to the tap (extension of the shoulder and elbow)
- Turns on the tap using the fingers (flexion and extension of the fingers and wrist movement)
- Places the hands under the water (elbow extension and forward movement of the shoulder)
- Rubs the palms and the backs of the hands (movement of the wrist and fingers)
- Extends the hands toward the soap and presses on it (extension of the shoulder and elbow, and flexion of the fingers)
- Rinses the hands with water (movement of the shoulder, elbow, and wrist)
- Raises the hands and dries them with the towel (elbow flexion and wrist movement)

#### Toothbrushing

- Reaches for the toothbrush and holds it (extension of the shoulder and elbow, and flexion of the fingers)
- Raises the brush to mouth level (flexion of the elbow and shoulder)
- Opens the toothpaste cap (movement of the fingers and wrist).
- Squeezes the toothpaste tube to put toothpaste on the brush (finger pressure)
- Places the brush inside the mouth (elbow flexion and wrist movement)
- Moves the brush over the upper and lower teeth (movement of the wrist and elbow)
- Raises the cup to the mouth to rinse (flexion of the elbow and shoulder)
- Returns the brush and the cup to their place (elbow extension and lowering of the arm)

## 2. Motor Planning

### First: Motor Planning within Dressing Skills:

#### 1. Putting on the T-shirt

- Identifies the orientation of the T-shirt and holds it at the appropriate place (spatial planning).
- Identifies the head opening and the sleeves before starting to dress (motor planning).
- Inserts the head through the opening intended for it, then inserts the arms into the sleeves (sequential planning).
- Pulls the T-shirt downward in a sequential, organised movement (temporal planning).
- Adjusts the position of the T-shirt until it settles on the body (motor planning).

#### 2. Putting on the Trousers

- Identifies the orientation of the trousers and the leg openings (spatial planning).
- Holds the trousers and prepares them for putting on (motor planning).
- Inserts the first foot and then the second foot into the appropriate openings (sequential planning).
- Pulls the trousers upward gradually until they reach the waist (temporal planning).
- Adjusts the position of the trousers around the waist (motor planning).

#### 3. Putting on the Jacket

- Identifies the front and the back of the jacket (spatial planning).
- Opens the jacket and prepares it for putting on (motor planning).
- Inserts the first arm into the sleeve and then the second arm (sequential planning).
- Pulls the jacket onto the shoulders and adjusts it (motor planning).
- Fastens the jacket from bottom to top in order (sequential planning).

#### 4. Putting on the Shirt

- Identifies the orientation of the shirt and its front and back (spatial planning).
- Inserts the arms into the sleeves in order (sequential planning).
- Adjusts the shirt on the body (motor planning).
- Begins fastening the buttons from top to bottom (sequential planning).
- Fastens the buttons one after another at a regular rhythm (temporal planning).

#### 5. Putting on the Socks

- Identifies the opening of the sock and its correct orientation (spatial planning).
- Opens the sock and prepares it for inserting the foot (motor planning).
- Inserts the toes first and then pulls the sock over the heel (sequential planning).
- Pulls the sock gradually until it reaches its correct position (temporal planning).
- Adjusts the position of the sock around the foot (motor planning).

#### 6. Putting on the Shoes

**The person with good performance:**
- Identifies the appropriate shoe for each foot (spatial planning).
- Opens the shoe and prepares it for inserting the foot (motor planning).
- Inserts the foot into the shoe in the correct way (motor planning).
- Adjusts the heel inside the shoe (spatial planning).
- Fastens the shoe using the hook-and-loop strap or the laces (sequential planning).

### Second: Motor Planning within Eating and Drinking Skills

#### 1. Eating a Sandwich

- Locates the sandwich and holds it at the appropriate part (spatial planning).
- Directs the sandwich toward the mouth (motor planning).
- Opens the mouth and takes the food (sequential planning).
- Moves the sandwich away after taking the bite (temporal planning).
- Repeats the eating steps until the sandwich is finished (sequential planning).

#### 2. Eating with the Spoon

- Locates the plate and the spoon (spatial planning).
- Directs the spoon toward the food (motor planning).
- Scoops a suitable amount of food (motor planning).
- Raises the spoon toward the mouth (motor planning).
- Takes the food and then returns the spoon to the plate (sequential planning).
- Repeats the steps at a regular rhythm until the food is finished (temporal planning).

#### 3. Eating with the Fork

- Locates the food and holds the fork (spatial planning).
- Directs the fork toward the piece of food (motor planning).
- Presses the fork onto the food to pick it up (motor planning).
- Raises the fork to the mouth (motor planning).
- Takes the food and then returns the fork to the plate (sequential planning).
- Repeats the steps at the appropriate speed (temporal planning).

#### 4. Drinking with the Cup

- Locates the cup and holds it (spatial planning).
- Raises the cup toward the mouth (motor planning).
- Adjusts the tilt of the cup to suit the amount of water (motor planning).
- Drinks and then returns the cup to its normal position (sequential planning).
- Places the cup on the table gently (temporal planning).

#### 5. Drinking with the Bottle

- Locates the bottle and the direction of its opening (spatial planning).
- Holds the bottle and prepares it for drinking (motor planning).
- Raises the bottle toward the mouth (motor planning).
- Tilts the bottle to the appropriate degree for drinking (motor planning).
- Lowers the bottle and returns it to its place (sequential planning).

#### 6. Drinking with the Straw

- Locates the cup and the straw (spatial planning).
- Places the straw inside the cup (motor planning).
- Directs the tip of the straw to the mouth (spatial planning).
- Places the straw between the lips and begins drinking (sequential planning).
- Finishes drinking, moves the cup away, and returns it to its place (temporal planning).

### Third: Motor Planning within Personal Hygiene Skills:

#### 1. Handwashing

- Goes to the sink and locates the tap (spatial planning).
- Turns on the tap and wets the hands (motor planning).
- Puts soap on the hands (sequential planning).
- Rubs the palms, between the fingers, and the backs of the hands (motor planning).
- Rinses the hands with water and then turns off the tap (sequential planning).
- Dries the hands with the towel (temporal planning).

#### 2. Toothbrushing

- Fetches the toothbrush and the toothpaste (motor planning).
- Puts the toothpaste on the brush (sequential planning).
- Identifies the areas of the teeth that will be cleaned first (spatial planning).
- Cleans the upper teeth and then the lower teeth (sequential planning).
- Moves between the right side and the left side and cleans all the teeth (spatial planning).
- Rinses the mouth, washes the brush, and returns the items to their place (sequential planning).

## 3. Motor Coordination

### 1. Dressing

#### a- Putting on the T-shirt

- Holds the two edges of the T-shirt with both hands to open it (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the T-shirt with one hand and guides the other arm into the sleeve (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the sleeve opening toward the arm while following the movement with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Inserts the arms and then pulls the T-shirt downward in sequential order (sequential motor coordination).

#### b- Putting on the Trousers

- Holds the two sides of the trousers with both hands and opens them (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the trousers with one hand and guides the foot with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the foot toward the trouser opening while following it with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Inserts the feet and then pulls the trousers upward in sequence (sequential motor coordination).

#### c- Putting on the Jacket

- Holds the jacket by both sides and spreads it out with both hands (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the jacket with one hand and guides the other arm into the sleeve (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Follows the sleeve opening and the position of the arm with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Inserts the arms and then adjusts the jacket on the shoulders (sequential motor coordination).

#### d- Putting on the Shirt

- Spreads out the shirt and holds its two sides with both hands (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the shirt with one hand and inserts the other arm into the sleeve (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the buttons toward the buttonholes and follows their position with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Inserts the buttons into the buttonholes in sequence using both hands (sequential motor coordination).

#### e- Putting on the Shoes

- Holds the shoe with both hands and opens it (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the shoe with one hand and guides the foot with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the foot into the shoe while following its position with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Adjusts the position of the shoe and fastens it if it has a lace or a hook-and-loop strap (sequential motor coordination).

#### f- Putting on the Socks

- Holds the two edges of the sock with both hands and opens it (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the sock with one hand and guides the toes with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the toes into the sock opening while following them with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Pulls the sock gradually until it covers the foot (sequential motor coordination).

### 2. Eating and Drinking

#### a- Eating the Sandwich

- Holds the sandwich with both hands (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the sandwich with one hand and adjusts its position with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the sandwich toward the mouth while following its position with the eyes (visual-motor coordination).
- Lifts the sandwich and brings it close to the mouth (sequential motor coordination).

#### b- Using the Spoon

- Stabilises the plate with one hand and holds the spoon with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the spoon toward the food inside the plate (visual-motor coordination).
- Picks up the food with the spoon and then raises it toward the mouth (sequential motor coordination).

#### c- Using the Fork

- Stabilises the plate with one hand and holds the fork with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the fork toward the piece of food (visual-motor coordination).
- Picks up the food with the fork and then moves it toward the mouth (sequential motor coordination).

#### d- Drinking with the Cup

- Holds the cup with both hands when needed (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the cup with one hand and lifts it with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the rim of the cup toward the mouth (visual-motor coordination).
- Lifts the cup, tilts it to drink, and then returns it to its place (sequential motor coordination).

#### e- Drinking from the Bottle

- Holds the bottle with both hands when needed (symmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the bottle with one hand and opens the cap with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the opening of the bottle toward the mouth (visual-motor coordination).
- Lifts the bottle, tilts it to drink, and then returns it (sequential motor coordination).

#### f- Using the Straw

- Stabilises the cup with one hand and holds the straw with the other (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the straw into the opening of the cup (visual-motor coordination).
- Places the straw in the cup and then directs it to the mouth (sequential motor coordination).

### 3. Personal Hygiene

#### a- Handwashing

- Uses both hands to turn on the water tap when needed (bilateral coordination).
- Stabilises the soap with one hand and rubs it with the other hand (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Rubs the palms of the hands, their backs, and between the fingers (sequential motor coordination).
- Directs the hands under the water to rinse and then uses the towel to dry (visual-motor coordination).

#### b- Toothbrushing

- Holds the toothbrush and stabilises the toothpaste tube with the other hand (asymmetrical bilateral coordination).
- Directs the toothpaste toward the bristles of the brush (visual-motor coordination).
- Squeezes the tube to put the toothpaste on the brush (motor coordination).
- Moves the brush over the teeth in a regular order (sequential motor coordination).

## 4. Midline Crossing

### 1. Dressing

#### a- Putting on the T-shirt

- The child extends the right hand across the middle of the body to reach the sleeve on the left side, and vice versa (midline crossing).
- Uses one hand to reach the edge of the T-shirt on the opposite side and adjust it (midline crossing).

#### b- Putting on the Trousers

- The child extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the opposite side of the trousers and adjust it (midline crossing).
- Uses the hand to reach the edge of the trousers on the other side while pulling them upward (midline crossing).

#### c- Putting on the Jacket

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the sleeve on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Reaches with the hand to the edge of the jacket on the other side to adjust it on the body (midline crossing).

#### d- Putting on the Shirt

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the opposite sleeve (midline crossing).
- Reaches with one hand to the buttons on the opposite side of the shirt (midline crossing).
- Reaches the opposite buttonhole to insert the button into it (midline crossing).

#### e- Putting on the Shoes

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the shoe located beside the opposite foot (midline crossing).
- Reaches with the hand to the outer part of the shoe to adjust its position if it is on the opposite side (midline crossing).

#### f- Putting on the Socks

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the sock located beside the opposite foot (midline crossing).
- Reaches with the hand to the edge of the sock on the opposite side to adjust it (midline crossing).

### 2. Eating and Drinking

#### a- Eating the Sandwich

- The child extends the right hand across the middle of the body to reach the sandwich on the left side, and vice versa (midline crossing).
- Moves the sandwich from one side of the body to the other side using the opposite hand (midline crossing).

#### b- Using the Spoon

- Extends the hand used for the spoon across the middle of the body to reach the plate placed on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Picks up the food from the plate on the opposite side and moves it to the mouth (midline crossing).

#### c- Using the Fork

- Extends the hand used for the fork across the middle of the body to reach the food on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Picks up the food from the plate on the opposite side and moves it to the mouth (midline crossing).

#### d- Drinking with the Cup

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the cup placed on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Holds the cup and moves it from the opposite side to the mouth (midline crossing).

#### e- Drinking from the Bottle

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the bottle placed on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Holds the bottle and moves it to the mouth (midline crossing).

#### f- Using the Straw

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the straw or the cup on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Stabilises the cup with one hand and reaches with the other hand to the straw if it is placed on the opposite side (midline crossing).

### 3. Personal Hygiene

#### a- Handwashing

- Uses the right hand to rub the back of the left hand, then the left hand to rub the back of the right hand, with crossing of the middle of the body when it actually occurs (midline crossing).
- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the soap or the towel placed on the opposite side (midline crossing).

#### b- Toothbrushing

- Extends one hand across the middle of the body to reach the toothbrush or the toothpaste placed on the opposite side (midline crossing).
- Moves the brush to the mouth, crossing the middle of the body if the placement of the items requires it (midline crossing).

## 4. Hand Skills (Reach, Grasp, Carry, and Release)

### 1. Dressing

#### a- Putting on the T-shirt

- Extends the hand toward the T-shirt to reach it (reach).
- Holds the two edges of the T-shirt with the fingers and the palm (grasp).
- Lifts the T-shirt and moves it toward the body (carry).
- Opens the hands and lets go of the T-shirt after securing it in place (release).

#### b- Putting on the Trousers

- Extends the hand to reach the trousers (reach).
- Holds the two edges of the trousers (grasp).
- Lifts the trousers and moves them toward the feet and the body (carry).
- Lets go of the trousers after adjusting them in place (release).

#### c- Putting on the Jacket

- Extends the hands to reach the jacket (reach).
- Holds the edges of the jacket (grasp).
- Lifts the jacket and moves it to the body (carry).
- Lets go of the jacket after putting it on and adjusting it (release).

#### d- Putting on the Shirt

- Extends the hand to reach the shirt and the buttons (reach).
- Holds the edges of the shirt and the buttons using the fingers (grasp).
- Lifts the shirt and moves it to the body (carry).
- Lets go of the shirt after adjusting it and fastening the buttons (release).

#### e- Putting on the Shoes

- Extends the hand to reach the shoe (reach).
- Holds the shoe by its edges (grasp).
- Lifts the shoe and moves it toward the foot (carry).
- Lets go of the shoe after putting it on and adjusting it (release).

#### f- Putting on the Socks

- Extends the hand to reach the sock (reach).
- Holds the two edges of the sock with the fingers (grasp).
- Lifts the sock and moves it toward the foot (carry).
- Lets go of the sock after pulling it on and securing it on the foot (release).

### 2. Eating and Drinking

#### a- Eating the Sandwich

- Extends the hand to reach the sandwich (reach).
- Holds the sandwich using the fingers and the palm (grasp).
- Lifts it and moves it to the mouth (carry).
- Lets go of it after placing it on the plate or when finished with it (release).

#### b- Using the Spoon

- Extends the hand to reach the spoon (reach).
- Holds the handle of the spoon (grasp).
- Lifts the spoon and moves the food with it to the mouth (carry).
- Leaves the spoon on the plate or the designated surface (release).

#### c- Using the Fork

- Extends the hand to reach the fork (reach).
- Holds the handle of the fork (grasp).
- Lifts the fork and moves the food to the mouth (carry).
- Lets go of the fork after using it (release).

#### d- Drinking with the Cup

- Extends the hand to reach the cup (reach).
- Holds the cup with one hand or with both hands (grasp).
- Lifts the cup and moves it to the mouth (carry).
- Returns the cup to its place and opens the hand to let go of it (release).

#### e- Drinking from the Bottle

- Extends the hand to reach the bottle (reach).
- Holds the body of the bottle (grasp).
- Lifts the bottle and moves it to the mouth (carry).
- Returns the bottle to its place and lets go of it (release).

#### f- Using the Straw

- Extends the hand to reach the straw (reach).
- Holds the straw with the fingers (grasp).
- Moves it to the cup and places it inside the liquid (carry).
- Lets go of the straw after placing it in the cup (release).

### 3. Personal Hygiene

#### a- Handwashing

- Extends the hand to reach the tap, the soap, and the towel (reach).
- Holds the tap handle, the bar of soap, and the towel (grasp).
- Moves the soap to the hands, and the towel to the hands when drying (carry).
- Leaves the soap and the towel in their place after use (release).

#### b- Toothbrushing

- Extends the hand to reach the toothbrush and the toothpaste (reach).
- Holds the toothbrush and the toothpaste tube (grasp).
- Lifts the brush and moves it to the mouth, and holds the toothpaste tube in order to use it (carry).
- Puts the brush and the toothpaste in their place and lets go of them after finishing (release).
