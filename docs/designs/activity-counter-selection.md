# Camera-counted activity selection

Story 4.4. Applies the design rule ("Camera-counted activity selection" in `daily-living-skills-app.md`): an activity is camera-counted only when its measurement criterion is a count of repetitions or a hold time of ONE body movement that ONE `angleAbove`/`angleBelow`/`pointAbove`/`distanceBelow` check describes. Source: `docs/reference/treatment-plans.md`. Anything ambiguous is excluded (guided). Data: `tools/catalog-builder/activity_counters.json`, validated against `tools/catalog-builder/schema/criterion.schema.json`.

Result: 5 included, 143 excluded (of 148).

## Threshold conventions

- `target`, `kind`, `perSide` are taken from the criterion text. `perSide: true` only when the text says "each arm/leg/hand/side/knee"; then left and right are counted separately, each to `target`.
- `perSide: true` requires `check.side: either`: left and right are counted separately and each side must reach `target`.
- Ranges: angle thresholds 0..180; distance ratios >= 0; `reps` targets are whole numbers. The check's `deg`/`ratio`/`margin` must equal the start threshold (`high` for angleAbove/pointAbove, `low` for angleBelow/distanceBelow); the builder enforces all of this.
- `low` < `high` are the counter's movement thresholds (degrees for angles, shoulder-width ratios for distances). For `angleAbove`/`pointAbove` a rep is above `high` then below `low`; for `angleBelow`/`distanceBelow` a rep is below `low` then above `high`. The check's `deg`/`ratio` equals the start threshold.
- All `low`/`high` values are **starting values to tune in replay** (Story 3.9 pipeline), not clinical content.

## Tuning notes

- `moderate.rom.02` (opening and closing the arms): with the arms fully open the wrists can leave the frame at the sides. Out-of-frame frames are ignored by the counter, so a rep whose open phase is never seen is not counted (and a gap over 250 ms resets the phase). Check framing and the `high` ratio in replay first.
